# Vendored packages

Nine upstream projects are copied into `packages/` because they are not on PyPI in a form we can depend on. Never edit them by hand; update them with the make targets below.

## LTX (MLX)

`packages/ltx_core_mlx` and `packages/ltx_pipelines_mlx` are vendored from [`dgrauet/ltx-2-mlx`](https://github.com/dgrauet/ltx-2-mlx). They are not published to PyPI, and PyPI rejects direct-URL dependencies, so they live in the repository. Never edit them by hand.

- **Pin.** `LTX_COMMIT` in the `Makefile` must be the commit of a release tag; the comment above it names the tag.
- **Update.** `make update-ltx` replaces both packages with the pinned commit. It does not carry their dependencies: mirror the `dependencies` of upstream's `packages/*/pyproject.toml` into ours by hand, macOS-only and capped at the minor version that upstream's `uv.lock` tests (for example `mlx-arsenal>=0.2.4,<0.3`).
- **Internals we rely on.** `backends/mac/video.py` uses upstream attributes and call shapes that are not public API. Re-check each on every bump. `tests/contracts/test_ltx_mlx_contract.py` pins their names and signatures by introspection, and the mocked tests in `tests/backends/mac/test_mac_ltx_video.py` cover our calls:
    - `_pending_loras` on the pipeline (LoRAs applied at load)
    - the environment variables `load_model` and `_generation_scope` default: `LTX2_LORA_MODE` (`unfused`), which the pipeline reads in its constructor, and `LTX2_COMPUTE_DTYPE` (`float16`), which it reads when it loads the transformer
    - the duck-typed `stepwise` slot and its `bind(..., stage=)` contract, which returns the stage's `on_step` callable
    - `_pre_denoise_flush(video_state, audio_state)` (guards against the Metal watchdog) and `_decode_and_save_video(frame_rate=, seed=)`
    - `dit`, `load()`, `prompt_encoder`, `video_patchifier`, `audio_patchifier`, `low_memory`, and `image_conditioner` (`__call__(fn, free_after=)` and `resolve_crf`)
    - the `gemma_model_id` constructor argument of `BasePipeline` and `DistilledPipeline`, which takes a local folder, and the names the loader picks from a pack (the transformer, the video decoder and encoder, the upsampler and its config, the audio files, the connector, the text encoder and the model config), which the pack `files` lists in `model_metadata.yaml` must contain
    - resetting `vae_decoder`, `audio_decoder` and `vocoder` to `None` to free the decoders
    - `utils._orchestration.combined_image_conditionings`, `utils.args.ImageConditioningInput`, and `utils.helpers.create_noised_state(legacy_scalar_blend=True)`
    - `ltx_core_mlx`'s `X0Model`, `snap_output_dimensions(two_stage=)`, `compute_video_latent_shape`, `compute_audio_token_count`, `compute_audio_positions`, `compute_video_positions` and `aggressive_cleanup`
    - `scheduler.shorten_schedule`, `DISTILLED_SIGMAS` and `STAGE_2_SIGMAS`, and `utils.samplers.denoise_loop` with its `on_step(step_idx, total, video_x0, sigma)` signature
    - for LTX-2.5 packs: `utils.generation.is_ltx25_pack` and `BasePipeline.model_dir`, `scheduler.LTX_2_5_DISTILLED_SIGMAS` and `LTX_2_5_STAGE_2_DISTILLED_SIGMAS`, `components.diffusion_steps.EulerAncestralDiffusionStep(eta, s_noise)`, `utils.samplers.euler_ancestral_denoising_loop` (keyword arguments `transformer=`, `stepper=`, `noise_seed=` and `on_step=`), `distilled.ANCESTRAL_ETA`, `ANCESTRAL_S_NOISE` and `ANCESTRAL_NOISE_SEED_OFFSET`
    - the two file rules `utils.model_files` mirrors: `ltx_core_mlx`'s `select_text_encoder` (a root `text_encoder.safetensors` with `text_encoder_config.json` marks a bundled Gemma 4 encoder) and `ltx_pipelines_mlx`'s `blocks._video_vae_names` (the conv decoder of 2.5 packs wins over the 2.3 one)
    - for review before upscaling: `_stage1` (shadowed per call), `Stage1Result.video_tokens`/`audio_tokens`/`latent_dims`, `_load_decoders`, `dit`/`_loaded` (restored after the preview decode), and the model state released after an aborted generation
    - Upscaling a video file (`LtxVideoBackend.upscale_video`): `generate_two_stage(stage1=, stage2_steps=)` and its resume path (`_stage1(resume=)`, which checks the arrays against the request), the `Stage1Result` constructor fields, `video_vae.tiling.TilingConfig`, `SpatialTilingConfig` and `TemporalTilingConfig` (keyword arguments), `VideoEncoder.tiled_encode(video, tiling_config)`, `_source_audio_tokens` on the audio-to-video pipeline's stage-2 audio state, and `STAGE_2_SIGMAS` having one more entry than the most refine steps
    - `DistilledPipeline.generate_and_save` for upscale, and `BasePipeline`'s `gemma_model_id` default, the transformer-file preference order and the `low_memory` and `low_ram_streaming` constructor arguments
    - Extending a clip (`LtxVideoBackend.extend_video`), video side: `conditioning.types.VideoConditionByLatentIndex(frame_indices, clean_latent, strength)` pins the carried tail at the start of the window; `retake.encode_video_tensor(encoder, video_tensor)` and `utils.media_io.video_preprocess(frames, height, width)` encode the tail frames inside `image_conditioner(fn, free_after=)`
    - Extending a clip, audio side: `ltx_core_mlx.utils.audio.load_audio(path, target_sample_rate, start_time, max_duration, mono)` reads the tail's sound at its position in the file; `audio_vae.encode_audio(waveform, sample_rate, encoder, processor)` encodes it inside `audio_conditioner(fn, free_after=)`; `extend_distilled.fit_audio_latent(audio_latent, audio_tokens)` fits the `(1, 8, T, 16)` latent before `audio_patchifier.patchify`, and `extend_distilled.carry_audio_tokens(frame_rate, carry_pixel_frames)` gives the token count
    - Extending a clip, decode: the pipeline's `_loaded` flag (cleared with `dit = None` so the next `load()` reloads the transformer), `video_decoder_block.load()` and `video_decoder_block.video_decoder` (the diffusion decoder is rejected), `video_vae.VideoDecoder.tiled_decode(latent, tiling_config)`, `video_vae._compute_decode_tiling(latent_shape, frame_rate, budget_bytes)` (which may return `None` or temporal tiles), `video_vae.decode_cache_limit()`, `video_vae.stream_chunks_to_ffmpeg(chunks, proc)` and `video_vae._ffmpeg_sink(cmd)`, and `audio_decoder_block.__call__(audio_latent)`
    - Extending a clip: `ltx_core_mlx.utils.audio.load_audio` returns an `AudioData` whose `.waveform` and `.sample_rate` are passed to `encode_audio`, and `video_patchifier.patchify` and `audio_patchifier.patchify` return `(tokens, dims)` tuples, so the tokens are `patchify(...)[0]`
- **Converted folders.** `backends/mac/ltx_converted.py` writes LTX checkpoints in the layout of dgrauet's MLX folders, which upstream loads: `transformer.`-prefixed names with the renames of `LTXV_LORA_COMFY_RENAMING_MAP`, the block Linears quantized at group size 64 (the bit width is read back from the shapes), `connector.`-prefixed connectors, and `embedded_config.json`. Everything else comes from the pinned pack of the checkpoint's level: the `files` of its pinned pack that do not start with `transformer`, fetched with `snapshot_with` and hard-linked into the folder, except `embedded_config.json` and `connector.safetensors`, which the converter writes or links itself. The pack is the one the version's `converter_base` names for the level (read in `utils/ltx_facts.py`). Re-check that layout against a published folder on every bump.
- **Cache limit.** `low_ram_streaming` makes the pipeline set the MLX buffer-cache limit to 0 for the whole process. `backends/mac/memory.mlx_cache_limit` scopes that to model construction and each generation, then restores the previous limit.

## SeedVR2

`packages/seedvr2_torch` is the PyTorch SeedVR2 inference core from [`numz/ComfyUI-SeedVR2_VideoUpscaler`](https://github.com/numz/ComfyUI-SeedVR2_VideoUpscaler) (Apache-2.0), which the Windows and Linux plain upscale runs. Upstream is not a pip package, so it lives in the repository. Never edit it by hand.

- **Pin.** `SEEDVR2_COMMIT` in the `Makefile` must be the commit of a release; the comment above it names the version.
- **Layout.** `make update-seedvr2` copies upstream's `src/` without `src/interfaces/` (the ComfyUI nodes), the two model configs, the fixed text embeddings `pos_emb.pt` and `neg_emb.pt`, and the licence, and writes its own `__init__.py` in place of upstream's, which loads the nodes. Upstream's code imports itself relatively and finds its configs and embeddings three folders above `src/utils/constants.py`, so the copy keeps upstream's layout and is imported as `seedvr2_torch.src`.
- **Update.** It does not carry dependencies: mirror upstream's `requirements.txt` into ours by hand, Windows and Linux only, leaving out `gguf` (only imported for GGUF weights) and `matplotlib` (debug output). `torchvision` comes from the same CUDA index as `torch`, and `opencv-python` is replaced by `opencv-python-headless`.
- **Internals we rely on.** `tests/contracts/test_seedvr2_torch_contract.py` pins the entry points the upscaler calls, their keyword arguments and the bundled files.

## MiniMax H3 MLX

`packages/minimax_h3_mlx` is the MLX pipeline for MiniMax H3 from [`Argus-AiTeam/minimax-h3-mac`](https://github.com/Argus-AiTeam/minimax-h3-mac) (Apache-2.0), which the macOS H3 backend runs. Upstream is not published to PyPI, so it lives in the repository. Never edit it by hand.

- **Pin.** `H3_MLX_COMMIT` in the `Makefile` names the vendored commit and the comment above it names the branch and date. `tests/contracts/test_h3_mlx_contract.py` checks it against `H3_MLX_COMMIT` in `backends/mac/h3_video.py`.
- **Layout.** `make update-h3-mlx` copies upstream's `minimax_h3_mlx/` without its command-line modules (`generate_cli.py`, `doctor.py` and `cleanroom_smoke.py`), adds `scripts/quantize_text_encoder.py` as `quantize_text_encoder.py`, and copies the license. Upstream imports itself absolutely as `minimax_h3_mlx`, so the directory keeps that name and is imported as a top-level package.
- **Update.** It does not carry dependencies: mirror upstream's `dependencies` into ours by hand, macOS-only. `mlx-vlm` is pinned exactly, because upstream imports its Qwen3-VL vision model, and a newer `mlx-vlm` pulls a newer `mlx` than the one LTX is tested with. Check that `uv lock` adds packages and leaves `mlx` where it was. On macOS the pinned `mlx-vlm` also needs a newer `starlette` (the Web UI's server library) than Windows and Linux use, so run the Web UI on a Mac after a bump.
- **Internals we rely on.** `tests/contracts/test_h3_mlx_contract.py` reads the source as a syntax tree, since Linux has no MLX, and pins the `MiniMaxH3Pipeline.from_pretrained` and `__call__` parameters, the `GenerationResult` fields, the low-memory image refusal, the `self` attributes and signatures of the pipeline methods `h3_staged` calls with a `SimpleNamespace` (`_row_timestep_plan`, `_encode_keyframes`, `_decode_video`, `_decode_audio`), the signatures of the packing, loading, streaming, scheduler, transformer and text-encoder pieces it composes, the text encoder's `_processor` getter, the mlx-vlm and transformers pieces it uses (`VisionModel.sanitize`, `VisionConfig.from_dict`, `LanguageModel.get_rope_index`, the PIL image processor), the Turbo LoRA key pattern and its six targets, and `quantize_text_encoder.convert`. Import checks for the same names run where MLX is installed.

## MOSS-Image

`packages/moss_image` holds the pipeline file of [`OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-Image-SFT`](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-Image-SFT) (Apache-2.0), which the Windows and Linux MOSS-Image backend runs. Upstream ships it as a file in a model repository, not as a package, so it lives in the repository. Never edit it by hand.

- **Pin.** `MOSS_REVISION` in the `Makefile` names the vendored revision of the SFT repository and the comment above it names the branch and date. The `sft` target of the `moss` entry in `model_metadata.yaml` must name the same commit, so the weights the builder downloads are the ones the pipeline code was written for. `tests/contracts/test_moss_contract.py` checks the `Makefile` revision against the one the generated `__init__.py` names.
- **Layout.** `make update-moss` downloads `pipeline_moss_image.py` and `LICENSE` from that revision, unchanged, and writes its own `__init__.py` naming the repository and revision. The folder is imported as the top-level package `moss_image`.
- **Update.** It does not carry dependencies: the pipeline needs only `torch`, `diffusers` and `transformers`, which are already ours. Check that those versions still import it.
- **Internals we rely on.** `tests/contracts/test_moss_contract.py` reads the source as a syntax tree and pins the classes `MOSSImageTransformer`, `MOSSImageScheduler`, `MOSSImagePipeline` and `_Block`; the transformer's constructor parameters and defaults and its `forward` signature; the `_Block.proj` keys; `MOSSImageScheduler.set_timesteps(num_inference_steps, device)`; the pipeline's constructor, the `__call__` keywords the backend passes, and `encode_prompt(prompt, max_sequence_length)` together with the `self` attributes it reads (`tokenizer`, `text_encoder` and `_execution_device`), because the backend runs it on a namespace that supplies only those. Several Linears (`layers.*.proj.q`, `text_embedder.proj`, `video_embedder`, `time_embedding.0`, `shared_block_modulation`, `video_head_modulation` and `final_linear_video`) read `weight.dtype` to choose the dtype of the activations, so quantized storage there corrupts them and they stay in the compute dtype. One import check proves the file loads against the installed `diffusers` and `transformers`.

## BiRefNet

`packages/birefnet` holds the model code of [`ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet`](https://huggingface.co/ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet) (MIT), which the background removal chain builds its mask network from. Upstream ships it as files in a model repository, not as a package. Never edit it by hand.

- **Pin.** `BIREFNET_REVISION` in the `Makefile` names the vendored revision. The `birefnet` target of the `matte` entry in `model_metadata.yaml` pins the weights to the same commit. `tests/contracts/test_birefnet_contract.py` checks that the two and the generated `__init__.py` agree.
- **Layout.** `make update-birefnet` downloads `birefnet.py` and `BiRefNet_config.py` from that revision, unchanged, and writes its own `__init__.py`. Upstream ships no license file, so none is copied; `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md` cites the model card. The folder is imported as the top-level package `birefnet`.
- **Update.** It carries no dependencies: the code imports `timm`, `kornia`, `einops` and `torchvision` besides `torch` and `transformers`, which `pyproject.toml` lists for every platform. Check that those versions still import it.
- **Internals we rely on.** The contract test reads the source as a syntax tree and pins `BiRefNet`, `BiRefNetConfig` (its `model_type` is `SegformerForSemanticSegmentation`, so it is never resolved through `AutoConfig`) and `DeformableConv2d`: its constructor parameters, its `offset_conv`, `modulator_conv` and `regular_conv` layers, the integer `padding` and the `2 * sigmoid` modulator, all of which the `grid_sample` replacement relies on. `Decoder` is defined twice upstream; the later definition is the one in use. One import check proves the file loads against the installed packages.

## YuE2

`packages/yue2` is the inference kit of [`multimodal-art-projection/YuE`](https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/YuE) (`src/yue2`, Apache-2.0), which the audio backend runs for music. Upstream ships it in its repository and as a wheel inside the model repository, not on PyPI. Never edit it by hand.

- **Pin.** `YUE2_COMMIT` in the `Makefile` names the vendored commit, and `make update-yue2` writes it to `packages/yue2/UPSTREAM_COMMIT`; `tests/contracts/test_yue2_contract.py` checks the two agree. Move the pin only after checking that a take is still bit-identical and fits the measured memory.
- **Layout.** `make update-yue2` copies `src/yue2/` without `cli.py` and `fast.py` (the command line and the vLLM backend), plus the repository's `LICENSE`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `licenses/`. The kit imports itself relatively and is imported as the top-level package `yue2`. `pipeline.py` imports `fast` only for `backend="vllm"`, which we never select. The weights are licensed separately (CC BY-NC 4.0) and are not vendored; `MODEL_LICENSE` stays upstream.
- **Update.** It does not carry dependencies: the kit needs `torch`, `transformers`, `huggingface-hub`, `safetensors`, `numpy` and `accelerate`, which are already ours (`soundfile` is imported only by the kit's unused `save` helper and is not installed), and `tiktoken`, which `pyproject.toml` lists. Check that the kit still imports and that a take is bit-identical to the stock kit's.
- **Internals we rely on.** `tests/contracts/test_yue2_contract.py` reads the source as a syntax tree and pins the pipeline entry points, keywords and private parts the CUDA weight swap hooks into, so an update that moves one fails there. One import check proves the package loads against the installed libraries.
- **The private call for Tempo and Key.** To plan from a written score header, `backends/audio/yue2.py` calls the kit's private `YuE2Pipeline._generate(prefix, sampling, seed, "abc", cancelled=, on_token=)` with `token_prefixes(request, tokenizer)` and the header's tokens as the prefix, `resolve_sampling` and `generation_config.abc` for the sampling, and then hands the finished score back through `plan(request=SongRequest(abc=score))`. `tests/contracts/test_yue2_contract.py` pins those signatures, the `SongRequest` fields and the `request.abc` path of `plan`, so an update that changes one fails there; check them (and that the kit still writes the `K:` line after the voice lines) before moving the pin.

## Stable Audio 3

`packages/stable_audio_3` is the inference package of [`Stability-AI/stable-audio-3`](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-audio-3) (MIT), which the audio backend runs for sound and ambience. Upstream is not on PyPI and pins `torch==2.7.1`, so it lives in the repository. Never edit it by hand.

- **Pin.** `SA3_COMMIT` in the `Makefile` names the vendored commit, and `make update-sa3` writes it to `packages/stable_audio_3/UPSTREAM_COMMIT`; `tests/contracts/test_stable_audio_3_contract.py` checks the two agree and that it is still the commit the audio backend was measured at.
- **Layout.** `make update-sa3` copies `stable_audio_3/` without `training/`, `interface/`, `cli.py` and `data/dataset.py`, plus the repository's `LICENSE`. `data/utils.py` stays because `inference/sampling.py` and `inference/audio_utils.py` import it. Nothing comes from Stability AI's gated model repository: the weights and configs are fetched separately. The package imports itself absolutely as `stable_audio_3`, so it is a top-level package.
- **Update.** It does not carry dependencies: the inference path needs `einops`, `safetensors`, `numpy`, `packaging`, `transformers`, `huggingface-hub`, `tqdm`, `torch` and `torchaudio` (imported at module level by `data/utils.py`), which `pyproject.toml` lists. `torchaudio` comes from the same CUDA index as `torch`. `flash_attn` is optional and absent. Check that `uv lock` adds nothing else.
- **Internals we rely on.** `tests/contracts/test_stable_audio_3_contract.py` reads the source as a syntax tree and pins the loader and `generate` signatures and the clamp after decoding that the backend works around. One import check proves the inference path loads without the excluded folders.

## Qwen3-TTS

`packages/qwen_tts` is the sources of the [`qwen-tts-hf`](https://github.com/andimarafioti/Qwen3-TTS/tree/distribution/qwen-tts-hf) 0.1.1.post1 wheel (Apache-2.0), the Transformers 5 build of Qwen's [`Qwen3-TTS`](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS), and `packages/faster_qwen3_tts` is [`andimarafioti/faster-qwen3-tts`](https://github.com/andimarafioti/faster-qwen3-tts) 0.5.4 (MIT). Together they run Qwen3-TTS speech on Windows and Linux. Both are on PyPI, but `qwen-tts-hf` pins `accelerate==1.12.0` and requires `gradio`, which no installer can resolve against our `accelerate`: a `[tool.uv] override-dependencies` entry works only inside this repository, not for the wheel that `uv tool install` resolves. Never edit them by hand.

- **Pin.** `QWEN_TTS_COMMIT` (branch `distribution/qwen-tts-hf`, the commit that sets version 0.1.1.post1) and `FASTER_QWEN3_TTS_COMMIT` (tag `v0.5.4`) in the `Makefile` name the vendored commits, and `make update-qwen3-tts` writes each to its `UPSTREAM_COMMIT`; `tests/contracts/test_qwen3_tts_vendored.py` checks the files agree with the `Makefile`. Both trees are identical to the installed wheels of those versions apart from the omitted modules.
- **Layout.** `make update-qwen3-tts` copies `qwen_tts/` without `cli/` and `__main__.py` (the demo, the only code that imports `gradio`) and `faster_qwen3_tts/` without `cli.py`, plus each repository's `LICENSE`. Both import themselves relatively and by their own name, so they are top-level packages. `qwen_tts/core/__init__.py` imports the 25 Hz tokenizer, which imports `sox` and `onnxruntime`, so both stay dependencies although the app only uses the 12 Hz tokenizer. `zvisiongenerator/backends/audio/qwen3_tts_shim.py` imports `qwen_tts` under `quiet_qwen_import` to keep the console free of the messages that `sox` and the `flash_attn` probe print.
- **Update.** It does not carry dependencies: mirror what the two packages import into ours by hand, non-macOS only: `librosa`, `soundfile`, `sox` and `onnxruntime` (`torch`, `torchaudio`, `transformers`, `huggingface-hub`, `numpy` and `einops` are already ours). Leave out `gradio`, and `qwentts-cpp-python` (imported only for the GGML backend, which we never select). After a bump, run `scripts/check_wheel_resolves.sh` (see [Releasing](releasing.md#wheel-resolve-check)).
- **Internals we rely on.** `tests/contracts/test_qwen3_tts_contract.py` pins the `FasterQwen3TTS` and `Qwen3TTSModel` signatures the backend calls and each part of the shim (the read-only Mimi properties, the global `"default"` RoPE entry the import adds, the missing conversion mapping of the custom encoder), so an update that moves one fails there.
